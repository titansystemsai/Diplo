Who we are

Defence projects don't run on theory.

DIPLO — Defence Industry Pathway & Leadership Outreach is a student-led initiative connecting university students from diverse academic disciplines with the practical realities of Australia's defence industry.

Through industry briefings, professional workshops, expert panel discussions and scenario-based learning, DIPLO bridges the gap between academic study and professional practice. Participants gain exposure to the professional standards, multidisciplinary collaboration and leadership capabilities that underpin complex, highly regulated defence environments, preparing them to transition confidently from university into industry.

Students get earlier visibility of the industry. The industry gets earlier visibility of work-ready students.

Industry exposure

Connect with defence professionals through industry briefings, expert panels, and networking opportunities. Gain insight into career pathways, multidisciplinary collaboration and the expectations of organisations operating across Australia's defence sector.

Professional capability

Develop the practical skills that underpin successful defence careers, including governance, communication, critical thinking, teamwork, decision-making and working within structured, highly regulated environments through realistic, industry-led scenarios.

Leadership & outreach

Build confidence in stakeholder engagement, professional conduct and leadership while contributing to a growing community that connects students, universities, and industry across a broad range of disciplines.

What DIPLO delivers

Ready students. A smarter pipeline.

For students

Develop industry-ready capability. Build the professional skills expected across the defence sector, including communication, collaboration, governance, critical thinking, and professional judgement through practical, scenario-based learning.
Learn directly from industry. Engage with defence professionals through briefings, workshops and expert panels that provide insight into real projects, career pathways and the expectations of complex, multidisciplinary organisations.
Build your professional network early. Establish meaningful connections with industry before graduation, demonstrate initiative beyond the classroom and position yourself for future opportunities across Australia's defence sector.

For industry partners

Engage with emerging talent. Connect with motivated students from engineering, project management, cyber security, business, information technology, law, logistics, finance, and other disciplines before they enter the graduate market.
A structured, low-commitment partnership. Contribute through industry briefings, workshops or panel discussions designed around your expertise, with clear objectives, minimal preparation, and no recruitment obligation.
Strengthen tomorrow's workforce. Support the development of career-ready graduates by helping bridge the gap between university education and the professional expectations of Australia's defence industry.
